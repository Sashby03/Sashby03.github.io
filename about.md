## Outside the classroom

When I’m away from class, I like hiking, training, and cooking new recipes. Living in the Pacific Northwest makes it easy to find a trail or a new place to explore.

Training in martial arts and grappling gives new challanges to overcome: staying calm under pressure, problem solving at a high pace, and creativity are all things i love about it. Cooking is another way I like to experiment. **I’m happiest when I’m learning something hands-on**, whether that means improving a technique, testing a recipe, or making a small creative project. 

### A few interests

- Cooking and trying new foods
- picking up new skills
