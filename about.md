## Outside the classroom

When I’m away from class, I like getting outside, training, and trying new recipes. Living in the Pacific Northwest makes it easy to find a trail or a new place to explore. I also enjoy creative coding and thinking about how ordinary objects and places might tell stories about the past.

Training in martial arts and grappling gives me a different kind of problem to work on: it takes patience, practice, and learning from small mistakes. Cooking is another way I like to experiment. **I’m happiest when I’m learning something hands-on**, whether that means improving a technique, testing a recipe, or making a small creative project. *I’m still a beginner in plenty of these areas, which is part of the fun.*

### A few interests

- Hiking and camping around Washington
- Muay Thai, wrestling, and Brazilian jiu-jitsu
- Cooking and exploring new foods
- Creative coding and archaeology
